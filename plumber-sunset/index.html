Sunset runs its own water, and North Davis Sewer District takes what leaves

Sunset City operates its own water utility and bills water, sewer and garbage together through the City Building. That makes the city meter the dividing line between the public system and your plumbing. Ask Sunset City where its responsibility ends and yours begins before you pay anyone to dig, because the pages we reviewed do not print that boundary.

Council minutes show an annual payment to Weber Basin Water for wholesale supply, so Weber Basin Water Conservancy District sits upstream of the Sunset system. Weber Basin reports a district-wide hardness range of 10 to 18 grains per gallon, or 171 to 308 parts per million. Sunset has not published a figure of its own. Test your tap and ask the city for its water quality report before you shop for a softener.

North Davis Sewer District handles the sewer side, with Clearfield, Clinton, Layton, Roy, Syracuse, West Point and Hill Air Force Base in the same district. The district lists wipes, including the ones sold as flushable, as a never-flush item. It also says fats, oils and grease plug sewer lines more than anything else. A main-line clog that keeps returning deserves a hard look at the kitchen drain and the toilet before anyone blames the pipe.

City council minutes also mention meter replacement and a Main Street water main replacement. Work on mains can push air or sediment into house lines. If faucets sputter or aerators clog afterward, a plumber can flush the lines and check the fixtures.

What the ground does under a Sunset yard

The Utah Geological Survey says shallow groundwater, generally within 10 feet of the surface, can occur anywhere in Utah. It is more likely in low valley areas and near waterways. The survey did not map Sunset, so we cannot say your lot is wet. Levels follow irrigation and rain, and the water can seep into basements. If a lower level smells damp or a sump pit fills every spring, have the pit checked before the wet season.

Cold is the other half of the ground story. NOAA normals for the Ogden airport station average 27.9 nights at or below freezing in December, 28.7 in January and 23.6 in February. Pipes in an unheated garage, an attic run, a crawlspace or the hose bibb on the north wall take the hit first. A split pipe often shows itself only when it thaws, so a wet ceiling the morning after a cold snap is a call, not a wait-and-see.

What Sunset homes call about most

Toilets lead the list. A toilet that runs between flushes is usually losing water past a worn flapper or fill valve, one that rocks has a failed seal at the flange, and one that overflows often has a blockage further down the line. The plumber checks the tank parts, the closet bolts and the wax seal, then decides whether a part or a new bowl makes sense. That is the work behind toilet repair.

Faucets and shutoff valves come next. A dripping cartridge faucet needs a new cartridge, and a corroded stop valve under the sink needs replacing so you can actually stop the water next time. A handle that will not shut the flow off is a same-day faucet repair job, not a weekend project.

If the meter keeps turning with every tap closed, the leak is somewhere between the meter and the fixtures. Because Sunset City owns the meter, the first question is which side of it the water is escaping from. That conversation sets up any water line repair.

Water heaters round out the list. Sunset's building page says a water heater, furnace, AC or meter upgrade needs a permit, listed at $50 plus a 1 percent state surcharge, and that plumbing work in a remodel needs one too. When a tank leaks from the base rather than a fitting, water heater replacement is the usual route and the permit belongs in the plan.

Before we arrive in Sunset

A few minutes of work before the plumber arrives changes how the visit goes, especially in a house that may be decades old.

1Find your house main

Look where the line enters the house, often a basement wall, a utility closet or a crawlspace. The city side of the meter belongs to Sunset City, so ask the city before touching its valve. If your house valve is seized, say so on the call.

2Cut power near water

Switch off the breaker for any room with a wet ceiling or dripping fixture. Keep everyone away from outlets, light fittings and the water heater area until it is dry.

3Clear the work space

Empty the cabinet under the sink and move boxes away from the water heater. Older homes often have tight utility closets, and a clear path saves the plumber time.

4Note who owns what

If you rent, have the landlord's approval ready. Note when it started, what you heard and whether the meter was moving. Those details point the plumber at the right part of the system.

In a Sunset house from the 1950s or 1960s the shutoff may not have been turned in years. Do not force a stuck valve, because a broken stem turns a leak into a flood.

Sunset plumbing questions, answered plainly

Is emergency service available in Sunset?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24/7, and licensed plumbers are sent to Sunset addresses for overflowing toilets, failed supply lines, leaking water heaters and sewer backups. Do you cover every ZIP code in Sunset?

Sunset has one ZIP, 84015, and it covers the whole city. The same ZIP also covers Clearfield, Clinton and West Point, so a ZIP match alone does not tell dispatch which town you are in. Give your street address and say Sunset on the call. The plumber is then routed from our Clearfield base to the north.

Who do I call if the leak is before the water meter?

Sunset City runs the water utility, so water on the street side of the meter is a city call. Water on your side is a plumber call. If unsure, close your house main and watch the meter. A meter that still turns means the leak may sit before your valve. In that case the city should hear about it.

Does a water heater replacement in Sunset need a permit?

Sunset's building page says a water heater, furnace, AC or meter upgrade needs a permit. Plumbing work in a remodel needs one too. The page lists a fee of $50 plus a 1 percent state surcharge. Fees change, so confirm the amount with the city before the work is scheduled.

Is a sewer backup the city's problem or mine?

North Davis Sewer District serves Sunset, but the pages we reviewed do not say who owns the line from the street to the house. Ask the city and the district. A camera shows where the blockage sits, and that tells you whether it is your pipe or the main. If every drain in the house gurgles, treat it as urgent.

Can a renter in Sunset call for a plumbing repair?

Yes. Call us to stop the leak, then tell your landlord right away. About 35 percent of Sunset homes are not owner-occupied, so this comes up often. We recommend closing the shutoff first and sorting out who pays after the water is under control.

Why does my toilet keep running in an older Sunset house?

Usually a worn flapper or a fill valve that no longer shuts off cleanly. In older homes the stop valve behind the toilet can also be corroded, which makes it hard to isolate the tank. A plumber can swap the parts, and if the valve is seized, replace it so the next repair is simple.

The water turned cloudy after city work on the lines. What now?

Air and loose sediment can enter house lines after mains work. Run a cold tap for a few minutes and remove the aerators, since sediment lodges there. If clouding lasts, or hot water stays rusty, call and the plumber can flush the lines and check the water heater, or contact the city about the work.
