Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Clearfield Express Plumbing may receive when you call us or visit expressplumberclearfield.com, and what we do with it. It is written in plain language and takes effect in 2026.

Who we are

Clearfield Express Plumbing is a plumbing dispatch service based at 1436 S Legend Hills Dr, Clearfield, UT 84015. We arrange for licensed plumbers to visit homes and businesses in Clearfield and nearby towns. Our phone number is (801) 207-7520 and our email is contact@expressplumberclearfield.com.

What a phone call may collect

Our business runs by phone. When you call, we may receive your phone number and the date, time and length of the call. We also record what you tell us so we can arrange service, such as your name, your address and a description of the plumbing problem.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call or end it. You can also ask us about the situation without sharing more than you are comfortable with.

How we use call information

We use call information to understand the problem, send a plumber, confirm a price with you, and follow up on the work. We may also review recordings and call notes to check that our team answered correctly and to train people who answer the phone.

We share the details a plumber needs, such as your address and the issue, with the licensed contractor assigned to your job. We do not use your details for anything unrelated to arranging and improving plumbing service.

What happens when you visit this website

This website has no contact forms, no accounts and no login. We do not ask you to type personal details into the site, and we do not offer checkout or online payment.

Like most websites, the servers that deliver our pages keep standard logs. These logs can include your IP address, the type of browser and device you use, the pages you request, and the time of each request. We use them to keep the site running, find errors and protect it from abuse.

Cookies and third party content

Pages that show a map load content from the map provider, which may handle your request under its own privacy terms. Your phone or browser may also store basic preferences. You can clear stored data in your browser settings at any time.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the licensed contractors who carry out your job, with service providers who help us run the phone line and website, and when the law requires it.

If our business changes hands, call records may transfer to the new operator under the same limits described here.

How long we keep information and how we protect it

We keep call records and notes for as long as we reasonably need them to handle your job, answer questions about it, and meet legal or accounting duties. After that we delete them or remove identifying details.

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute protection.

Your choices

You can ask what call information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and we will respond as the law allows. Send the request to contact@expressplumberclearfield.com or call (801) 207-7520, and tell us the phone number and date of the call so we can find it.

Our services are meant for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time, and the version on this page is the current one. The effective date is 2026.

Questions about this policy can go to contact@expressplumberclearfield.com, or you can call (801) 207-7520. Our address is 1436 S Legend Hills Dr, Clearfield, UT 84015.
