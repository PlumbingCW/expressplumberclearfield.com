What downtown Clearfield is, and how this page uses the name

Clearfield City's downtown development page says downtown was a hub of activity from the 1950s to the 1990s, and it shows Center Street in a 1959 photograph. The city says the town became fully built out, that economic downturns closed influential businesses, and that market challenges stalled new housing.

The city then started over on paper. It completed three studies between 2010 and 2016, wrote a downtown code between 2016 and 2018, and has been building from 2018 onward.

The city speaks of a downtown corridor and publishes no street-by-street boundary, so this page uses State Street and Center Street near City Hall as its reference. We go by the street address you give.

What is verified here, and what is not

City Hall is at 55 S State Street, Clearfield, UT 84015. Clearfield City runs its own culinary water system through Public Works, and North Davis Sewer District lists Clearfield among the cities it serves. The city says most repairs and alterations need a permit.

No source we found says what pipe material is inside any downtown building, so this page makes no claim about the age or condition of its plumbing. The plumber looks at the actual pipes at your address.

Source: clearfield.city downtown development, Public Works and Business and Development pages, and North Davis Sewer District.

Redevelopment sites downtown customers mention

The city names three key revitalization spots: Clearfield Station, Lakeside Square and Lotus Anthem, and says it worked one on one with their property owners from 2019 to 2023. The Clearfield Station page covers the district around the FrontRunner stop.

Lakeside Square sits on the west side of Mabey Pond, and the city's project list shows 296 residential units in its northern section. In a managed apartment building, a ceiling leak can involve shared lines the owner controls, so tell the property manager first.

Services downtown customers ask about most

Drain cleaning clears a single blocked line, and hydro jetting scours a line that keeps clogging, such as a kitchen branch. No hot water points to water heater repair, and a damp wall or a rising bill points to leak detection.

Permits and who to ask

Clearfield's Business and Development page says most repairs or alterations require a permit. It does not single out water heater swaps or sewer work, so we do not state a rule for them. Ask Clearfield City Community Development at City Hall, 55 S State Street, and ask the plumber how the permit will be handled.

Questions about plumbing in Downtown Clearfield

Which streets count as Downtown Clearfield?

The city refers to a downtown corridor and publishes no exact edges. We use State Street and Center Street near City Hall as the reference, and go by the street address you give.

What ZIP code is Downtown Clearfield?

City Hall at 55 S State Street uses ZIP 84015, the standard Clearfield ZIP. Clearfield also has PO Box ZIP codes that have no street delivery, so give your street address, not a ZIP, when you call.

Do you work on shops, offices and restaurants downtown?

Yes. Blocked drains, leaks and water heaters are handled the same way as home calls. See commercial plumbing.

Are the pipes in older downtown buildings worn out?

We cannot say. No source we found describes the plumbing inside any specific downtown building. The plumber inspects the pipes at your address.

Who do I call about a sewer backup downtown?

North Davis Sewer District says to contact it about backups on district-owned lines. Which part of a line belongs to the owner is not stated on the pages we found, so call us with your address and we will look at the building side.

Do I need a permit for plumbing work in Clearfield?

Clearfield says most repairs or alterations require a permit. For a specific job, ask Clearfield City Community Development at City Hall.
