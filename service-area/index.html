Our base address and what it means for coverage

The dispatch address is 1436 S Legend Hills Dr, Clearfield, UT 84015. Legend Hills Drive runs along the southeast side of the city, close to the Layton line, so Layton is the nearest large town to the base. Clearfield also has two PO Box ZIP codes, 84016 and 84089. They carry mail, not street delivery, so dispatch for drain cleaning, leak detection or a water heater repair always goes by the street address you give.

A base address matters because plumbers start their routes from somewhere. Being in Clearfield puts Layton, Sunset, Clinton and Syracuse next door, and Roy a few miles north. It does not mean a plumber is sitting at the address, since dispatch works by assigning the nearest available licensed plumber.

How dispatch reaches north Davis County and Roy

Davis County towns are served in a loop around the base. Layton lies south and southeast, Syracuse and West Point are west, Clinton sits northwest, and Sunset borders Clearfield on the north.

Roy is the exception because it is in Weber County and does not touch Clearfield directly. Sunset and Hill Air Force Base lie between the two, so a call to Roy usually travels up through that corridor on I-15 or the state routes beside it. Roy is a large town, so it gets full service and its own page.

All of this is run from one 24/7 line. Call (801) 207-7520, give the street and the problem, and the dispatcher assigns a plumber.

Places we mention but do not list as towns

Hill Air Force Base sits just northeast of Clearfield and borders Layton, Sunset and Roy. It is federal land with its own housing management, so we treat it as a landmark and not a market. If you live in a home just outside the gates, our dispatch to Layton, Sunset or Roy covers you.

West Point sits west of Clearfield and uses the 84015 ZIP code too. Hooper is a Weber County city with the 84315 ZIP. Neither has its own page, so call (801) 207-7520 and ask if your address falls inside our dispatch area.

What near means when you call

Near means a plumber can reasonably be dispatched to your street from the Clearfield base. It does not mean a set arrival time, because traffic on I-15, State Street and Antelope Drive, plus whatever the plumber is already finishing, changes it hour by hour.

When you call, we will tell you what we know about availability and offer a same-day appointment when one is open. We do not guarantee drive times, and you should be wary of any plumber that does.

Services most requested across the area

Service area: what homeowners ask

Do you serve my town if it is not on this list?

Possibly. The five towns above have their own pages, but dispatch also reaches nearby addresses such as West Point and the area around Hill Air Force Base.

Call (801) 207-7520 with your street address, and the dispatcher will confirm whether a plumber can come.

Is Roy really part of your service area?

Yes. Roy is in Weber County and sits north of Clearfield beyond Sunset and Hill Air Force Base. It has its own page at Roy plumbing and uses ZIP 84067.

Dispatch handles it the same way as Davis County towns, through the same 24/7 line.

Why do Clinton, Sunset and Clearfield share a ZIP code?

The Post Office assigned ZIP 84015 to the Clearfield delivery area, and Clinton and Sunset are accepted place names inside it. Clinton and Sunset are separate cities, but the ZIP code covers all three.

That is why we ask for a street address and not just a ZIP when you call.

Do you charge extra to come to a town farther from Clearfield?

Pricing depends on the job and gets confirmed before work starts. Distance is one part of how a visit is planned, but the quote you hear is the price you agree to or decline.

Just ask the dispatcher on the call. They can explain what applies to your address.

Can you reach Hill Air Force Base or on-base housing?

We list Hill Air Force Base as a landmark and not a town, and on-base housing is managed by the installation, so ask when you call. Homes around the base in Layton, Sunset, Clearfield and Roy are within normal dispatch.

If you are unsure which category your home falls into, call and ask.

Is there an office I can visit?

The Legend Hills Dr address is our dispatch base in Clearfield, and service is arranged by phone. Please call (801) 207-7520 before visiting so someone can help.
