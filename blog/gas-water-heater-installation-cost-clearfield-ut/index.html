Gas Water Heater Installation Cost in Clearfield, UT: What Sets the Price

Gas water heater installation cost in Clearfield, UT depends far less on the tank than on what surrounds it: the vent, the gas piping, the permit, the strapping and the condition of the old connections. Two homes with the same heater can land at very different totals.

We do not print dollar figures here because honest numbers need a look at your closet. This page shows which factors move the price and what a clear written quote should list.

By the Clearfield Express Plumbing teamPublished October 15, 20267 min read

Why the same heater costs different amounts

A like for like water heater installation in the same spot, with a sound flue and a gas shutoff that still turns, is the cheap end of this job. Everything that departs from that pattern adds labor and parts. A tank that moves to a new wall, a vent that must be rerouted or a gas line that needs a bigger run each add a separate line to the quote.

That is why a phone number for the unit alone tells you little. The heater is the part you can see in a store, and it is rarely the part that surprises people.

If you are choosing between a tank and a tankless unit, the venting and gas sizing differences are the real cost drivers, not the shell of the heater.

The factors that build a gas install quote

These five items explain nearly every difference between two written quotes.

The heater itself
Tank size, efficiency class and whether it is a standard atmospheric unit, a condensing unit or a hybrid. A condensing unit adds a condensate drain and different venting.
Venting
The flue must terminate outdoors and rise without sags. A cracked or corroded vent is replaced before the new unit is trusted to it.
Gas piping and shutoff
A new flexible connector, a leak test of every joint and sometimes a larger line. This is where a licensed plumber is required, not optional.
Permit and inspection
Clearfield City lists water heater installations and replacements as permit work. Ask what the permit step adds to your quote.
Strapping, pan and relief pipe
Seismic straps, a drain pan where the floor needs one, and a relief valve discharge pipe that runs to a safe place.

Insurance usually pays for sudden and accidental water damage, such as a tank that bursts, and not for a heater that simply wore out. Check your own policy before you assume either way.

Rebates that change the bottom line in Clearfield

Enbridge Gas runs the ThermWise appliance rebate program, and its rebate page lists separate amounts for ENERGY STAR certified gas storage tanks, gas tankless units, condensing storage units and gas hybrids. The listing covers Utah, Idaho and Wyoming and shows no end date, so confirm the current amount for your unit before you count on it.

Claims must be postmarked within six months of installation. Keep the invoice and the model plate photo, because the rebate follows the equipment, not the plumber. A rebate is worth chasing only if the unit you buy actually qualifies, so ask for the model number on the quote.

Source: UTILITY, Enbridge Gas ThermWise appliance rebates page (enbridgegas.com/utwyid/save-money/thermwise).

What a clear written quote should list

Ask for these line items. A quote that skips them is hiding the parts that cause change orders.

  • The exact heater model number and size
  • Whether the vent is reused or replaced, and why
  • New gas connector and a leak test of the joints
  • The permit and inspection step, and who handles it
  • Seismic strapping, relief valve and discharge pipe
  • Haul-away of the old tank
  • Whether an expansion tank is needed on your system

If any of these shows as an allowance rather than a price, ask what triggers the real number.

When a low quote should worry you

A bargain number that omits the permit, the strap kit or the gas leak test is not a bargain. Utah licensing rules keep work on a gas appliance outside the minor-repair exemption, so the person doing the install should hold the right license. You can look any plumber up with the Utah Division of Professional Licensing.

Clearfield sits in seismic design category D or D1, and a heater that is not strapped is a hazard in an earthquake. Treat straps as part of the price, not a favor.

Older homes tend to hide the expensive branch

About 39 percent of Clearfield homes were built before 1980, and the median home dates to 1989. Closets in older houses often hold an undersized gas branch or a vent that was never lined, so ask for it to be checked on the visit.

The same pattern shows up in Syracuse and Layton, where newer subdivisions usually cost less to vent and older ones cost more.

Cost questions we hear most

Does Enbridge Gas offer a rebate for a new gas water heater in Clearfield?

Through its ThermWise program, yes. The rebate page lists amounts for ENERGY STAR gas storage, tankless, condensing and hybrid units, and requires the claim to be postmarked within six months of installation.

Confirm the current amount for your model before you buy.

Why do quotes for the same gas heater differ so much?

The difference is almost never the tank. It is the vent work, the gas line, the permit step and whether the quote includes straps and haul-away.

Ask each plumber to itemize those lines so you compare like with like.

Do I need a permit to install a gas water heater in Clearfield?

The city lists water heater installations and replacements as permit work, and gas lines are on that list too. Permit rules and fees change, so ask the Clearfield City building department.

Ask your plumber whether the quote includes the permit step.

Can a tankless heater use my existing gas line?

Often it cannot. The Department of Energy notes that the in-house gas line for a tankless unit will in most cases need to be three quarters of an inch in diameter, and sizing follows the manufacturer and code.

A condensing tankless unit also needs a condensate path and venting listed for it.

Is a more efficient gas heater worth the extra cost?

DOE reports that water heating is about 14 to 18 percent of a utility bill. Higher efficiency lowers that share, but it also tends to add vent and drain work to the install.

Weigh the rebate, the venting change and how long you will stay in the house.

Is the work safe to do myself?

Not for a gas unit. Utah's exemption for minor repairs excludes work on a gas appliance, so a licensed plumber is the safe route.

If you smell gas at any point, leave and call 911.
